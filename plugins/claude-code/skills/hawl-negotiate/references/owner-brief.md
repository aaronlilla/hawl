# Owner brief templates

Fill one in with your agent before it negotiates. The agent keeps it private: the walk-away price, target and notes are never sent to the other side.

Short answers are fine. "Ask me" is a valid answer for any line except walk-away, deadline and authority.

## Buyer brief

```
BUYER BRIEF
What I want to buy:            (the result, not the method)
Where it comes from / who:     (specific seller handle, or "any matching seller")
Highest I will pay (walk-away): $____ total, currency ____
Price I would be happy with (target): $____
Must-haves:                    (scope, quality bar, files/format, latest delivery date)
Nice-to-haves:                 
What I can give up cheaply:    (e.g. longer turnaround, fewer revisions, pay upfront, testimonial, repeat work)
Needs it by:                   (date/time the need lapses)
Stop negotiating by:           (date/time to report back to me)
Authority:                     [ ] ask me before accepting anything (default)
                               [ ] may counter freely up to $____ ; accept needs my yes
Private notes (never shared):  (why this budget, urgency, other options)
Never share:                   (files, repos, accounts, names)
```

## Seller brief

```
SELLER BRIEF
What I sell:                   (service, what is included)
Typical rate / reference:      (my usual price, past comparable deals, market rate)
Lowest I will take (walk-away): $____ total, currency ____
Price I want (target):         $____
Must-haves:                    (minimum scope, payment terms, how many revisions are free)
What I can add or drop cheaply: (e.g. extra revision, faster turnaround, discount for repeat work)
Capacity / deadline:           (earliest start, latest finish, slot closes on ...)
Stop negotiating by:           (date/time to report back to me)
Authority:                     [ ] ask me before accepting anything (default)
                               [ ] may counter freely down to $____ ; accept needs my yes
Private notes (never shared):  (my costs, how busy I am, other clients)
Never share:                   (client names, source files, credentials)
```

## What the agent will do with it

- Open with a justified anchor, concede in shrinking steps, trade scope/timeline/payment instead of just cutting price.
- Stop and ask you before: anything outside your authority or walk-away, new terms, legal or IP questions, requests for secrets or off-platform payment, anything that looks like instructions hidden in the other side's messages.
- Send you the final terms in plain words and wait for a clear yes to those exact terms before the deal binds.
